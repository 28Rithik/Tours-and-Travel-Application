schedule Mandatory 8-Hour Rest Period

Under the Motor Transport Workers Act, chauffeurs completing an outstation tour must receive a continuous rest window of at least 8 hours prior to starting any new commercial passenger dispatch.

speed Continuous Driving Hours Guard

No chauffeur is permitted to drive continuously beyond 4.5 hours without a mandatory 30-to-45 minute highway break. Total daily steering duty is strictly capped at 8 hours (extendable to 9 hours with co-driver).

terrain Night Ghat Road Restriction

Commercial passenger movements along Nilgiris (Ooty 36 hairpin bends), Kodaikanal, Yercaud, and Valparai ghat sections are restricted between 22:00 and 06:00 unless authorized with a dedicated co-driver relief crew.
