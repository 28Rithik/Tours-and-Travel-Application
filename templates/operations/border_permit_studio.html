local_police Commercial RTO Permit Master Classification & Fleet Guidelines

Standard operational permit matrix under Section 88 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 for Tamil Nadu commercial tourist and passenger fleet operators.

1. Contract Carriage Permit

Tourism Backbone

The entire vehicle is hired as a whole by one customer, group, or corporate client. Cannot pick up random passengers along the route. Fare is fixed for the entire journey.

Fleet: Sedan, Innova Crysta, Tempo Traveller, Luxury Coaches.

2. Tourist Permit

Sightseeing

Formally classifies the vehicle as a commercial tourist vehicle. Authorizes package tours, devotional pilgrimages, hill station excursions, and airport transfers.

Requirement: Mandatory authorization to legally market a vehicle for tourist hire.

3. All India Tourist Permit (AITP)

Nationwide Interstate

MoRTH consolidated annual authorization permitting seamless travel across all Indian states without paying separate state border road taxes at check-posts.

Benefit: Fast-track clearance through Walayar, Attibele, Naraharipet with zero border road tax.

4. Motor Cab Permit

≤ 6 Passengers

For commercial passenger vehicles carrying up to 6 passengers (excluding driver). Covers airport transfers, city local taxi services, and family tours.

Fleet: Swift Dzire, Toyota Etios, Innova Crysta, Maruti Ertiga.

5. Maxi Cab Permit

6 to 12 Passengers

For medium commercial passenger vehicles carrying 6 to 12 passengers. Extremely common in Ooty, Kodaikanal, and Munnar hill station holiday tourism.

Fleet: Force Urbania, Tempo Traveller 9/12 Seater.

6. Omnibus Permit

> 6/12 Passengers

For large commercial passenger vehicles carrying more than 6/12 passengers. Operates group tours, college IV industrial visits, school excursions, and marriage convoys.

Fleet: 18-Seater Mini Bus, 25-Seater Coach, 35/50-Seater Luxury Pushback Coaches.
