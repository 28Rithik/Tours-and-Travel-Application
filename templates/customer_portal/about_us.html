OUR JOURNEY & ROOTS

Built on Punctuality, Safety, and Direct Honest Pricing

Founded in 2011 in the industrial heart of Coimbatore, Sivagayathiri Travels and Transport LLP began with a clear purpose: to bridge the gap between unorganized road travel and premium corporate transit standards.

What started as a modest tourist taxi and mini bus service has steadily grown into a 320+ commercial fleet operation. Today, we handle daily tech park staff commutes, campus school bus networks, university Industrial Visits (IVs), and thousands of unforgettable holiday expeditions across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh.

CORE PHILOSOPHY

The Four Pillars of Our Operations

How we maintain unwavering quality and passenger satisfaction across every single kilometer.

Safety Without Compromise

Every commercial vehicle is fitted with electronic speed governors, dual CCTV cameras, fire suppression gear, and emergency exit doors verified before every departure.

📡

24x7 Mission Control Desk

Our centralized depot in Gandhipuram, Coimbatore continuously tracks active fleet positions, speed metrics, and traffic bottlenecks with real-time satellite telemetry.

We own and manage our core fleet. Customers deal directly with the transport operator, eliminating middleman broker commissions and unexpected surcharge surprises.
